# YAQEEN Advisory: Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** a Y whose right arm becomes a brass check-mark: certainty, verified.
- **Versions:** horizontal (primary) · stacked · symbol only · wordmark only.
- **Files:** `yaqeen-horizontal.svg`, `yaqeen-stacked.svg`, `yaqeen-symbol.svg`, `yaqeen-wordmark.svg` are the full-colour masters. `symbol/`, `horizontal/`, `stacked/`, `wordmark/` hold black, white and one-colour (teal) versions as SVG and PNG. `web/` holds favicon, app and PWA icons. `*-reversed.svg` are for dark backgrounds.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone equal to **the width of the Y's stem** (1x) on all sides. It scales with the logo. Never use a fixed distance.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 90 px wide | 22 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (use `yaqeen-symbol-small.svg` below 32 px) | 5 mm |

Below the minimum, drop "ADVISORY" and use the symbol or wordmark alone.

## 4. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yaqeen Teal | #0F3D3E | 15, 61, 62 | 76 / 2 / 0 / 76 | Primary: Y, wordmark, text |
| Brass | #B98B3E | 185, 139, 62 | 0 / 25 / 67 / 27 | The tick, small accents |
| Paper | #F6F3EC | 246, 243, 236 | 0 / 1 / 4 / 4 | Backgrounds |
| Slate | #5B6B6C | 91, 107, 108 | 16 / 1 / 0 / 58 | Secondary text |

CMYK values are conversions. Ask the printer for a proof or Pantone match before a large print run.

**Approved pairings:** full colour on white or Paper · reversed (white + brass) on Teal · black or one-colour Teal on white for stamps, invoices and fax-style documents · white on Teal for one-colour reversed use.

**Accessibility:** Teal on white is 11.95:1 (AAA). Brass on white is only 3.1:1, so use brass for the tick and large graphics, never for body text.

## 5. Typography
The wordmark is drawn as paths (geometric capitals). It is not a font and must never be retyped. For documents, pair it with a neutral humanist or geometric sans you have licensed for commercial use. Check the licence before choosing one.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch or squash · don't recolour outside the palette · don't put the brass tick on Teal-on-Teal or on busy photos · don't rotate · don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects · don't separate the tick from the Y · don't rearrange the lockup · don't retype the wordmark.

## 7. Open items
- Trademark search in India (and the GCC states you plan to register in) before launch.
- An Arabic-script wordmark (يقين) needs a properly designed or licensed Arabic typeface, so it was not drawn here.
- Stroke-to-path is already done (all artwork is filled paths). Ask a print vendor for PDF/EPS if they need it.
